Forex & Stock Market Tracker

Live Forex · Stocks · Crypto — powered by 100% free public APIs

🟠 Crypto via CoinGecko 💱 Forex via Frankfurter (ECB) 📊 Stocks via Yahoo Finance

Forex & Stock Market Tracker brings live financial market data directly into your Odoo backend — no API subscriptions, no credit card, no limits for standard usage. It connects to three fully free public APIs and refreshes prices automatically every 30 minutes.

✨ Key Features

💱 Forex Rates

EUR/USD · GBP/USD · USD/JPY · USD/CHF and any ECB-supported pair. Powered by the Frankfurter API — official ECB data, zero cost.

📊 Stock Prices

AAPL · TSLA · MSFT · NVDA and any NYSE/NASDAQ ticker. Powered by Yahoo Finance — real-time quotes, no key needed.

🟠 Crypto Prices

BTC · ETH · SOL · BNB · DOGE and 10+ more. Powered by CoinGecko — free tier supports all standard coins.

🔄 Auto-Refresh

Built-in cron job refreshes all symbols every 30 minutes automatically. Or use the manual "Refresh All" button anytime.

📋 Kanban Dashboard

Live price cards with ▲ green / ▼ red direction indicators, high/low, and last-updated timestamp.

📈 Price History

Every refresh snapshot is stored. View as a list or line graph chart to track price movement over time.

📸 Screenshots

📊 Kanban Dashboard — Live Price Cards

All tracked symbols at a glance — colour-coded by price direction, grouped by type.

☰ Symbols — List View with per-row Refresh

Full sortable list with price, change %, high/low, last update, and a per-row refresh button.

📈 Price History — Line Graph

Every price snapshot stored and visualised as a line chart per symbol.
